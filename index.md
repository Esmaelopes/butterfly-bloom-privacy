# Privacy Policy — Butterfly Bloom

**Last updated: November 4, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Butterfly Bloom is a mobile game developed for Android.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Butterfly Bloom, including through third-party advertising services.

**Developer:ELV Creative Motion  
**Privacy contact:elvcreativemotion@gmail.com

## 2. Information We Collect

Butterfly Bloom does not require players to create an account or provide their name, email address, or other personal information to play.

However, third-party services integrated into the game, including Google AdMob, may automatically process information such as:

- Device identifiers, including advertising identifiers where available and permitted.
- IP address and approximate location derived from it.
- Device information, operating system, and application information.
- Advertising interactions and diagnostic information.

The information processed depends on the device, applicable laws, consent choices, and service configuration.

## 3. Advertising

Butterfly Bloom uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process certain information to deliver, measure, and protect advertisements, subject to applicable privacy requirements and user choices.

For more information, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Butterfly Bloom requests consent before using certain advertising technologies.

Users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland may be presented with a consent form powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Where applicable, users can accept, refuse, or manage their consent preferences.

Users can revisit their available privacy choices through the game's privacy settings.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Butterfly Bloom is designed to provide an accessible and family-friendly gaming experience.

We take children's privacy seriously. If the game is made available to children, advertising and data-processing features will be configured according to applicable children's privacy laws and Google Play policies.

The game does not intentionally request personal contact information directly from children.

Parents or guardians with privacy concerns may contact us using the address below.

## 6. Data Sharing

Information processed by Google AdMob may be shared with Google and advertising technology partners, as described in their applicable privacy policies and consent disclosures.
